# TEJAS '26 — The Fresh Bloom · MCA Freshers

Official Freshers Party portal for **Srusti Academy of Management & Technology**, Department of Computer Applications.

## 🌟 Features Implemented:
1. **Accurate TEJAS '26 Visual Design**:
   - Palette: Imperial Maroon (`#5b0e23`), Radiant Gold (`#d4af37`), Silk Champagne (`#fdebb2`), Warm Cream Canvas (`#f7f1e6`).
   - Typography: *Cinzel* for majestic title, *Alex Brush* for graceful cursive script, *Fraunces* for editorial highlights, *Outfit* for crisp modern UI.
   - Traditional Indian Odissi dancer and celebratory graduate crowd line-art flourishes.

2. **3D Curved Panoramic Faculty Carousel**:
   - Glowing golden oval platform base with perspective depth.
   - Cylindrical 3D perspective curvature showcasing all **22 Real Faculty Members** of Srusti Academy.
   - Active center card elevation with glowing halo.
   - Interactive chevron navigation buttons (`‹` and `›`), drag/touch swipe, and keyboard left/right arrow controls.
   - Clicking any card rotates the carousel to center it; clicking the active center card opens the **Faculty Tribute Modal**.
   - Bottom badge: `22 Our Esteemed Teachers — Guiding, Inspiring, Always Supporting`.

3. **Authentic Event Schedule**:
   - Centered golden mandala divider.
   - 6 detailed timeline rows with time duration pills (`30 MIN`, `15 MIN`, etc.) and category badges.
   - High-resolution card imagery (Auditorium, Odissi blessing troupe, Keynote podium, Refreshments, Freshers Spotlight, and Concert).
   - Interactive session notes modal via `"View event notes >"`.
